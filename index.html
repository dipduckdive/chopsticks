How to play
  • Each hand starts with 1 finger. On your turn, tap one of your hands, then tap any other hand (yours or the computer's) to add your hand's finger count to it.
  • If a hand reaches 5 or more fingers, it's knocked out and can't be used or targeted.
  • If you have only one hand left and it holds an even number of fingers, you can split it in half between both hands instead of attacking, bringing your dead hand back.
  • Knock out both of the opponent's hands to win.

You win!
